## Charter s. 2(b) — Expression
Official text: "Everyone has the following fundamental freedoms: ... (b) freedom of thought, belief, opinion and expression, including freedom of the press and other media of communication."
Texte officiel : « Chacun a les libertés fondamentales suivantes : ... b) liberté de pensée, de croyance, d'opinion et d'expression, y compris la liberté de la presse et des autres moyens de communication ».
Plain language: You can share your views, hold signs, chant and speak. This protects peaceful expression, not violence or threats.

## Charter s. 2(c) — Peaceful assembly
Official text: "Everyone has the following fundamental freedoms: ... (c) freedom of peaceful assembly."
Texte officiel : « Chacun a les libertés fondamentales suivantes : ... c) liberté de réunion pacifique ».
Plain language: You have the right to gather with others peacefully in public. The key word is peaceful.

## Charter s. 1 — Reasonable limits
Official text: "The Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms guarantees the rights and freedoms set out in it subject only to such reasonable limits prescribed by law as can be demonstrably justified in a free and democratic society."
Texte officiel : « La Charte canadienne des droits et libertés garantit les droits et libertés qui y sont énoncés. Ils ne peuvent être restreints que par une règle de droit, dans des limites qui soient raisonnables et dont la justification puisse se démontrer dans le cadre d'une société libre et démocratique. »
Plain language: Rights are not unlimited. Laws can set reasonable limits, for example for public safety, traffic or noise.

## Charter s. 10 — Rights on arrest or detention
Official text: "Everyone has the right on arrest or detention (a) to be informed promptly of the reasons therefor; (b) to retain and instruct counsel without delay and to be informed of that right ..."
Texte officiel : « Chacun a le droit, en cas d'arrestation ou de détention : a) d'être informé dans les plus brefs délais des motifs de son arrestation ou de sa détention; b) d'avoir recours sans délai à l'assistance d'un avocat et d'être informé de ce droit ... »
Plain language: If you are detained or arrested, you have the right to know why, and to talk to a lawyer without delay.

## FAQ: Do we need a permit?
A Charter right to assemble does not remove city rules. Halifax Regional Municipality (HRM) may require a permit or booking for events on municipal property, especially with stages, amplified sound, tents or road closures. Check HRM's current special-event requirements early. (Links to: Charter s. 2(c), Charter s. 1)

## FAQ: Can I film police?
In general, filming in a public place is a form of expression. Film from a safe distance, do not physically interfere with police work, and follow lawful directions. If an officer asks you to stop or step back, stay calm and ask what law applies. (Links to: Charter s. 2(b), Charter s. 1)

## FAQ: What if I'm detained?
Stay calm and do not resist. Ask: "Am I being detained, and why?" You have the right to be told why and to speak to a lawyer without delay. You can say you wish to remain silent until you speak to a lawyer. Tell your legal observer. (Links to: Charter s. 10)

## FAQ: What if they ask for my name?
In many everyday situations you do not have to give police your name, but there are exceptions (for example if you are being ticketed, arrested, or driving). Ask if you are being detained. Never give a false name: that can be an offence. If unsure, ask for a lawyer and tell your legal observer. (Links to: Charter s. 10)

## FAQ: Can police tell us to move?
The Charter protects your right to gather peacefully, but that right has reasonable limits, like safety and traffic. So police can sometimes direct a crowd. Stay calm, ask which law or bylaw applies, follow directions for safety, and tell your legal observer. (Links to: Charter s. 2(c), Charter s. 1)

## FAQ: Can we use amplified sound?
Expression is protected, but city noise rules can still apply (a reasonable limit). HRM may have rules on amplified sound, times and volume. Ask HRM whether your sound plan needs approval, and turn sound off by the agreed time. (Links to: Charter s. 2(b), Charter s. 1)
